Use: REFERENCE ONLY. S2 is evidence about stroke order and direction, never a
source of shapes. Nothing from it is traced, embedded, cropped or copied into
this repository: evidence.json records, in our own words and data, what page 1
shows for each form (stroke numbers, arrows, joining marks, the continuity
reading of each segment, and how S2's structure compares with S1's). It holds
no S2 artwork, no image crops and no coordinates taken from S2.

The readings were made in Phase 1 of the cursive page work by two independent
readers per half of the alphabet, who agreed on 61 of 63 forms; a third reader
resolved к and Ю.
